Every benefit, explained.

Here is exactly what your Passport includes — and what founding members get at Full Access, for the Standard price.

Preferred Rates
Best Available Rate or Better
Members access the best available room rate at every partner destination — often at a discount not available through any other channel. Rates vary by property; specific discounts are detailed in the Passport member portal.
Room Benefits
Upgrade, Early In, Late Out
Room upgrades upon availability, complimentary early check-in, and late check-out at every partner destination — requested at booking, confirmed by the property's guest relations team.
On-Property Credits
F&B Credits & Dining Discounts
Many partner properties include a daily food and beverage credit — typically $100–$200 USD per stay — plus discounts at on-property dining venues and spas. Specific credits are listed for each destination in the Passport portal.
Arrival Experience
VIP Treatment Upon Arrival
Properties are notified of Passport member arrivals in advance. Welcome amenities, priority check-in, and dedicated attention from guest relations are standard across the collection.
Format
Small-Group, Destination Travel
Privé Retreats bring 8–16 players together at a partner destination for 3–5 days of coached padel, competition, and curated resort access. Each retreat is designed around the destination — what you play is shaped by where you are.
Access
