When does my Passport activate?
Immediately after checkout. You'll receive your Passport credentials by email and can begin booking member rates at partner destinations the same day.
What happens after the 200 spots fill?
The founding offer closes permanently. The standard Full Access rate is $995/year. Founding members keep their $595 rate for as long as their membership is active — it will never increase.
What are Privé Locations?
Privé operates its own padel venues in Montauk, NY and Miami, FL. Members receive member rates included for up to four visits per month — public day rates apply after that. Full Access members play at member rates year-round with no visit limit.
Can I cancel?
Yes. Privé Passport is an annual subscription. You can cancel before your renewal date and will retain access through the end of your paid period. Questions? info@prive-padel.com
