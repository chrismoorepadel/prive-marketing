Last questions, answered.

Can I cancel?
Yes. It's an annual membership through Stripe — cancel any time before renewal and keep full access through the period you've already paid for.
When do my benefits start?
Immediately. Your Passport activates the moment you join, and you can book at member rates the same day through your member portal.
What's the difference between Standard and Full Access?
Both carry the full network — 40+ destinations at member rates, partner clubs, and Privé Experiences. Standard includes member pricing at Privé Montauk and Miami for four visits a year; Full Access makes that unlimited, year-round, and adds first invitations to every retreat.
How do I actually use it?
You book through your member portal; your rate and member benefits are applied automatically, with any on-arrival perks flagged per property. Questions? info@prive-padel.com
