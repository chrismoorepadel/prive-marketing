Leading clubs in four cities. Stay at a paired Privé Destination and you play as a member of the club for the day.

Four cities

LIVE PARTNERS

Four cities.

LOS ANGELES

Padel Up.

Paired with The Maybourne Beverly Hills and Fairmont Century City.

Six indoor courts in West Hollywood. The first dedicated padel club in Los Angeles.

NEW YORK

Padel Haus.

Paired with The Surrey.

Three locations in Brooklyn — Dumbo, Williamsburg, and Greenpoint.

LONDON

Racketeer.

Paired with The Savoy and St James's Hotel and Club.

Rooftop courts in Soho's Kingly Court. Members-room culture in the heart of central London.

HONG KONG

Go Park.

Paired with Rosewood Hong Kong.

A multi-court facility at Kai Tak Sports Park, Hong Kong's largest sports development.

Member access

A court, already arranged.

Partner club access isn't a separate membership or a booking you chase. It comes with the stay, and it's arranged for you.

01
Book a paired destination

Each partner club is paired with Privé Destinations in the same city — The Savoy with Racketeer in London, Rosewood Hong Kong with Go Park at Kai Tak.

02
Your court is arranged for you

Booking is handled between the hotel concierge and the Privé Padel Passport team — you don't chase the club or the court yourself.

Passport is the Privé Padel membership: member rates and benefits at 40+ padel destinations, partner clubs, and our own courts in Montauk and Miami.

03
Play as a member for the day

Member pricing on court, and full access to the club's facilities while you're there — you arrive as a member rather than a visitor.

PRIVÉ PASSPORT

The court is part of the trip.

Partner club access is included with every Passport — when paired with a stay at a Privé Destination in the same city.
