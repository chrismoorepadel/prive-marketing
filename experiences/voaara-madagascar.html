Off the northeast coast of Madagascar, on its own stretch of white sand between a protected reef and the Indian Ocean, sits Voaara — a barefoot private-island resort with oceanfront villas, a beach club, and a padel court almost nobody on earth has played. For one week in September, the island belongs to Privé: seven days of padel, reef, and whale season, with everything from the first welcome cocktail to the farewell dinner already taken care of.

Seven Days

The week, day by day.

Day 1 — Arrival
Check in, find your villa, and meet the group over a welcome cocktail at Franco's Bar. Welcome dinner at La Plage by Sarrion.
Day 2 — The North Reef
The week's rhythm begins: half the group tours the property and snorkels the north reef while the other half takes the court — then you swap. Lunch at Le Grill; dinner at La Plage.
Day 3 — The Natural Pool
Quad bikes through the island interior to a natural pool, padel in rotation, and a castaway taco picnic on the sand.
Day 4 — Île aux Nattes
A full day on the tiny island off the island: the pirate cemetery and church, an oyster experience, and lunch at Fafana.
Day 5 — Whale Season
September is the height of humpback season in the Sainte-Marie channel. Whale watching by boat, padel in rotation, and movie night with pizza on the beach.
Day 6 — South Reef & Sunken Dinner
Quads to the mangroves, snorkeling the south reef, cocktails at the Bird's Nest — and a beach sunken dinner as the island tournament begins.
Day 7 — Tournament & Farewell
Final matches, final swims, and a farewell dinner at La Plage by Sarrion.
Day 8 — Departure
Transfers to the airport.
The Padel Program

An hour a day, under the palms.

Every guest receives an hour of padel per day with the dedicated Privé professional traveling with the group — technique in the morning, matched play in the afternoon, and a closing tournament under the palms. The program splits into small groups so court time is never crowded and no one misses the reef.

The Island

A barefoot private island, yours for a week.

Private Beach
A kilometre and a half of private white sand facing the warm Indian Ocean, entirely exclusive to Voaara — morning swims, evening cocktails, beachside dinners, and the June–October humpback migration.
Cultural Experiences
Guided visits that connect guests with Malagasy tradition — local villages, the island's pirate cemetery, and the lively Ambodifotatra market.
Water Activities
Snorkelling, paddle boarding, wing-foiling, deep-sea fishing, kayaking, and scenic beach hikes with gourmet picnics — all included in the rate.
