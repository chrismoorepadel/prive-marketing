Five days here, with everything handled, is the trip padel players tell stories about for the rest of their lives.

Five Days

The week, day by day.

Day 1 — New Horizons
Arrival at Tambolaka and transfer to Nihi; lunch at Nio Beach Club, Happiest Hour at the Boathouse, and a welcome dinner at Ombak.
Day 2 — Horses & Waterfalls
A bareback ride through the shallows of Nihiwatu beach as the tide recedes — Nihi's signature — then the Lapopu waterfall by trek or e-bike, with a picnic and a swim at its base. Padel in the golden hour.
Day 3 — The Spa Safari
A morning journey by coastal trek or open-air safari drive to a treehouse breakfast above the ocean, followed by treatments in a private bale. Group yoga at the Shalla; padel before Happiest Hour.
Day 4 — Sumba
The Sumba Foundation's clinics and schools — the island's story beyond the resort — an afternoon on court, and a private dinner under the stars.
Day 5 — Fond Farewells
A last breakfast at Nio Beach Club and transfers to Tambolaka.
The Padel Program

The most photographed court in the world.

The jungle court at Nihi may be the most photographed padel court in the world for a reason. An hour of private instruction per guest per day with the traveling Privé pro, open play at golden hour, and matches scored to the sound of the surf.

The Resort

The Edge of Wildness, yours for five days.

Nihiwatu Beach
A 2.5-kilometre private stretch of white sand — and the access point for Occy's Left, one of the world's most coveted surf breaks, limited to twelve surfers a day.
Nihioka Spa
Open-air pavilions in a sheltered creek framed by pandanus trees. The signature Spa Safari takes guests to a secluded valley of bamboo-clad treatment bales above the rice paddies.
Nio Beach Club
Casual beachfront dining, the resort's cliffside pool, and the evening gatherings that end each day above the Indian Ocean.

Getting there

Fly to Bali (DPS), then one hour on to Tambolaka (TMC). Transfers between Tambolaka and Nihi are included on arrival and departure. We're happy to advise on the best routing from your city.

Good to know
Occy's Left is one of the world's great waves. Sessions are limited to twelve surfers a day and bookable directly with Nihi.
Warm and green at the start of the island's lusher season. The program flexes with the tides — the horse ride in particular is tide-dependent.
The bareback experience at Nihiwatu carries a maximum rider weight of 85kg, and runs on the day's tide.
