Court & Cellar

Four days · Château de Lussac · Wine immersion among the Saint-Émilion vineyards

Fifteen minutes from Saint-Émilion, in the heart of the Bordeaux vineyards, Château de Lussac has been making wine since 1876 — a 30-hectare estate of classical architecture, a courtyard of honor, a wooded park, and now a padel court set directly among the vines. Court & Cellar is four days there during harvest season: mornings on court, afternoons in the region's icons, evenings at the château table.

Four Days

The retreat, day by day.

Day 1 — The Estate
Arrival and welcome drink, lunch at the château, the vineyard and cellar tour, your first padel session among the vines, and dinner at the château table.
Day 2 — Bordeaux
Morning padel at UCPA Sport Station on the river, lunch at Le Gabriel, and a guided tour of the UNESCO-listed city.
Day 3 — The Atlantic
Padel at Padel Touch on the Bassin d'Arcachon, lunch at La Corniche, and the climb up the Dune du Pilat — Europe's tallest sand dune.
Day 4 — Saint-Émilion
The medieval village and its monolithic church, a Grand Cru château visit and tasting, and departure.
The Padel Program

Four days, three courts, one pro.

Play moves with the itinerary — the estate court set among the vines, Bordeaux's most modern sports complex on the river, and the leading club on the Bassin d'Arcachon — with an hour of private instruction per guest each day from the dedicated Privé professional traveling with the group.

The Estate

A working château, making wine since 1876.

Vineyard & Cellar
Walking tours through 30 hectares of working vines, private cellar tours, and tastings of the estate's labels — including the flagship Lussac-Saint-Émilion.
The Court in the Vines
A single padel court set within the estate grounds — the only court in the Saint-Émilion appellation, and the home of Court & Cellar's estate sessions.
Estate Pool
A private pool within the château's grounds, framed by gardens and the surrounding vineyard landscape — yours for the length of the stay.
