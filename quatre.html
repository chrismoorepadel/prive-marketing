Ask about your project ↗
What determines the cost of a court project?+

Court quantity and configuration, site conditions, supporting works, delivery location and installation requirements all inform the proposal. Share your property details so we can define the scope and prepare the right next step.

Can Quatre handle the complete project?+

Yes. Our services cover planning, court manufacturing, logistics, site preparation, installation and operational consulting. The proposal sets out your court specifications, supporting works and project responsibilities.

Can an existing tennis court be converted?+

We can assess an existing court or sports surface as part of project planning. The available area, surface condition, base requirements and local project requirements determine the suitable approach.

Do I need a finished design to get started?+

You can come to us with an early idea or a project ready for a court proposal. If you need feasibility or detailed planning work, we agree a paid consulting scope and upfront fee before that work begins. An inquiry does not commit you to a paid engagement.

Can you work with our architect or builder?+

Yes. We work with property teams, architects, builders and project managers to integrate the court with the wider development. Tell us about your client’s project.
