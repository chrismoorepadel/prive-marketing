Ask about your project ↗
What determines the cost of a court project?+

The main factors are the number of courts, the court specification, the site work and the delivery location. We’ll review those details with you before preparing a proposal.

Can Quatre handle the complete project?+

Yes. We can handle planning, manufacturing, delivery, site preparation and installation. Your proposal will set out what’s included and the responsibilities of Quatre and your property team.

Can an existing tennis court be converted?+

We can assess your existing court for conversion. We’ll need to check the available space, the condition of the surface and base, and any local requirements before recommending an approach.

Do I need a finished design to get started?+

No. You can start with an idea, a site or completed plans. If the project needs a detailed feasibility study or other consulting work, we’ll agree the scope and fee upfront.

Can you work with our architect or builder?+

Yes. We can work alongside your architect, contractor or project manager. Tell us who is involved and how far along the project is. Discuss a client project.
